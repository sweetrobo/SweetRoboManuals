## Step 7: Prepare Ice Cream Mix

**🚨 CRITICAL:** Never power on without mix - causes "core board error"!**Mix ratio:** 1 bag (1.5kg powder) + \~3.5L water per batch**Use mixing bucket:** Combine ingredients in the 10L graduated bucket**Whisk thoroughly:** Use provided whisk to ensure no lumps remain**Prepare both batches:** F2 requires mix for both hoppers before powering on**Check consistency:** Mix should be smooth and uniform

## Step 8: Load Hoppers

**Open hopper lids:** F1: single hopper / F2: both Left and Right hoppers**Pour slowly:** Add prepared mix carefully to avoid splashing**Minimum fill:** 2 liters per hopper (must fill all hoppers on your model)**Maximum capacity:** 12 liters per hopper**Check fill level:** Keep below square openings on white tubes (for air flow)**Close lids:** Secure hopper lids after filling![Hopper setup showing proper fill levels and lid positioning](../.gitbook/assets/hopper-setup.webp)
