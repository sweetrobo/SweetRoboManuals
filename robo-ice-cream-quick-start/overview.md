# Setup Overview

<div class="setup-overview">

<h1>Robo Ice Cream F1/F2</h1>
<div class="subtitle">Quick Start Setup Guide</div>

<div class="setup-flow print-two-column">

<div class="setup-step">
<div class="setup-step-number">1</div>
<h3>Unbox & Inspect</h3>
<p>Check for damage, verify contents, and confirm machine remained upright during transport</p>
</div>

<div class="setup-step">
<div class="setup-step-number">2</div>
<h3>Position Machine</h3>
<p>Move to location, level the machine, and verify required clearances</p>
</div>

<div class="setup-step">
<div class="setup-step-number">3</div>
<h3>Interior Check</h3>
<p>Remove packaging, inspect components, and locate accessory kit</p>
</div>

<div class="setup-step">
<div class="setup-step-number">4</div>
<h3>Install Hardware</h3>
<p>Attach LED panels (optional) and install cup tubes with cups</p>
</div>

<div class="setup-step">
<div class="setup-step-number">5</div>
<h3>Sanitize System</h3>
<p>Clean hoppers with food-grade sanitizer and rinse thoroughly</p>
</div>

<div class="setup-step">
<div class="setup-step-number">6</div>
<h3>Prepare Mix</h3>
<p>Mix ice cream powder with water according to ratios</p>
</div>

<div class="setup-step">
<div class="setup-step-number">7</div>
<h3>Load Hoppers</h3>
<p>Fill both hoppers with prepared mix (minimum 2L each)</p>
</div>

<div class="setup-step">
<div class="setup-step-number">8</div>
<h3>Power On & Test</h3>
<p>Connect power, start system, and make your first ice cream</p>
</div>

</div>

<div style="margin-top: 3rem; padding: 1.5rem; background: #e6f3ff; border-radius: 12px;">
<h3 style="color: var(--primary-blue); margin-top: 0;">⚠️ Critical Requirements</h3>
<div style="text-align: left; max-width: 600px; margin: 0 auto;">
<p><strong>Power:</strong> 220V/20A dedicated circuit with NEMA 6-20R outlet</p>
<p><strong>Mix:</strong> Never power on without ice cream mix loaded</p>
<p><strong>Transport:</strong> Machine must remain upright at all times</p>
</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 2rem; color: #666; font-size: 0.9rem;">
<p>Estimated setup time: 60-90 minutes</p>
<p>Works with both F1 (single hopper) and F2 (dual hopper) models</p>
</div>

</div>
