Unbox & Inspect

Check for damage, verify contents, and confirm machine remained upright during transport

Key Actions: Visual inspection for shipping damage, inventory verification against packing list, ensure upright transport orientation was maintained throughout delivery process.

Position Machine

Move to location, level the machine, and verify required clearances

Requirements: Minimum 6" clearance on all sides, level surface within 1°, access to 220V/20A dedicated circuit with proper outlet configuration.

Interior Check

Remove packaging, inspect components, and locate accessory kit

Inspect: Remove all protective packaging materials, verify internal components are secure, locate and inventory accessory kit contents.

Install Hardware

Attach LED panels (optional) and install cup tubes with cups

Components: LED panel installation (if purchased), cup tube assembly, cup loading and alignment verification, secure all connections.

Sanitize System

Clean hoppers with food-grade sanitizer and rinse thoroughly

Process: Use food-grade sanitizer solution, clean all food-contact surfaces, rinse thoroughly with potable water, ensure no residue remains.

Prepare Mix

Mix ice cream powder with water according to ratios

Ratios: Follow manufacturer's mixing ratios precisely, use cold water (below 40°F), mix thoroughly until completely dissolved, strain if necessary.

Load Hoppers

Fill both hoppers with prepared mix (minimum 2L each)

Filling: Minimum 2L per hopper for proper operation, maximum fill line marked, ensure even distribution between hoppers for F2 models.

Power On & Test

Connect power, start system, and make your first ice cream

Testing: Verify 220V power connection, system startup sequence, run initial test cycle, quality check first output, adjust settings as needed.

Critical Requirements

⚡ Power Requirements

220V/20A dedicated circuit with NEMA 6-20R outlet. Standard 110V household outlets will NOT work.

🥤 Mix Requirements

Never power on without ice cream mix loaded. Minimum 2L per hopper required for safe operation.

📦 Transport Requirements

Machine must remain upright at all times. Tilting can damage internal components and void warranty.
