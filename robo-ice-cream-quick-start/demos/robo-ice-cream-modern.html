Robo Ice Cream Quick Start Guide

Robo Ice Cream F1/F2 Quick Start Setup Guide

Critical Requirements

Power Requires 220V/20A dedicated circuit
Mix Never power on without ice cream mix in hoppers
Transport Machine must remain upright at all times
1

Unbox & Inspect

Carefully remove the machine from packaging and inspect for any shipping damage. Check that all components are included and undamaged before proceeding.

2

Position Machine

Place the machine in its final location with adequate ventilation space. Ensure the surface is level and can support the machine's weight (approximately 150 lbs).

3

Interior Check

Open the machine and verify all internal components are properly secured. Check for any loose parts or damage that may have occurred during shipping.

4

Install Hardware

Install the mixing blades, dispensing valves, and any other removable components according to the included hardware guide. Ensure all connections are secure.

5

Sanitize System

Run the sanitization cycle using approved cleaning solution. This ensures the machine is hygienically ready for food service and removes any manufacturing residues.

6

Prepare Mix

Prepare your ice cream mix according to manufacturer specifications. Ensure the mix is at the proper temperature and consistency before loading into hoppers.

7

Load Hoppers

Fill both hoppers with prepared ice cream mix. Check that hoppers are properly seated and sealed to prevent leaks during operation.

8

Power On & Test

Connect to 220V power source and turn on the machine. Run initial test cycles to verify proper operation and dispense quality before opening for service.

Estimated setup time: 60-90 minutes
Works with both F1 and F2 models
