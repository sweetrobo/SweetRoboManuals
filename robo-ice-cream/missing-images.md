## Photography Tips

When capturing these images:
1. Use consistent lighting and background
2. Include hands/tools where it shows process
3. Ensure F2 model-specific features are visible
4. Capture from operator's perspective
5. Show safety equipment where relevant
6. Ensure all text/labels are readable
7. Capture both wide shots and close-ups where noted

- Use lowercase with hyphens
- Be descriptive but concise
- Include section prefix when helpful
- Always use .webp format after conversion
